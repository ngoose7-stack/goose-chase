How to play

A mystery city is hidden somewhere in the lower 48 states (plus DC). You start in a random big city. Find it in as few flights as you can, landing as close to it as you can each time.

  • Type any city and press Fly. Typing a state (or its two-letter abbreviation) lists its cities.
  • Each flight gives you a clue. If the city you flew to shares something with the mystery city's name, you get a name clue:
    • Same number of letters → you learn the letter count
    • Same first letter(s) → you learn how it starts
    • Same last letter(s) → you learn how it ends
    • Once you know the count, letters in matching positions are revealed
  • If there's no new name clue, you get a direction instead (e.g. "northeast of Denver"), and that part of the map is shaded out.
  • The ? button has extra hints and a give-up option.
  • Scoring: each flight's miss is how far it lands from the mystery city. Your total miss sets your accuracy: 100% for a first-flight bullseye, 50% at 2,300 mi total miss. Giving up scores 0%.

Spaces and punctuation don't count as letters. The daily puzzle is the same for everyone each day.
