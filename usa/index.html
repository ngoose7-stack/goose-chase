How to play

You've been dropped in a random city. Find your way to the mystery city by guessing, using the map, and using clues revealed by guessing.

  • Type any city and press Fly. Typing a state lists its cities.
  • Every flight tells you which direction the mystery city is from where you landed (e.g. "northeast of Denver"), and that part of the map is shaded out.
  • If the city you flew to also shares something with the mystery city's name, you also get a name clue:
    • Same number of letters → you learn the letter count
    • Same first letter(s) → you learn how it starts
    • Same last letter(s) → you learn how it ends
  • The ? button has extra hints and a give-up option.
  • Scoring: each flight's miss is how far it lands from the mystery city. Your total miss informs your accuracy score (from 0% to 100%).
