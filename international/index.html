How to play

You've been dropped in a random city. Find your way to a mystery city by guessing, using the map, and using clues revealed by guessing.

  • Type any city and press Fly. Typing a country lists its cities.
  • Every flight tells you which direction the mystery city is from where you landed (e.g. "northeast of Cairo"), and that part of the map is shaded out.
  • If the city you flew to also shares something with the mystery city's name, you get a name clue too:
    • Same number of letters → you learn the letter count
    • Same first letter(s) → you learn how it starts
    • Same last letter(s) → you learn how it ends
    • Once you know the count, letters in matching positions are revealed
  • The ? button has extra hints and a give-up option.
  • Scoring: each flight's miss is how far it lands from the mystery city. Your total miss sets your accuracy: 100% for a first-flight bullseye, 50% at 8,500 mi total miss. Giving up scores 0%.

Spaces and punctuation don't count as letters. The daily puzzle is the same for everyone each day.
